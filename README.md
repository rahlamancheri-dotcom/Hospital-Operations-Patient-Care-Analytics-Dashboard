# Hospital-Operations-Patient-Care-Analytics-Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Hospital Operation & Patient Care Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based dashboard developed to analyze hospital operations and patient care performance.

The dashboard transforms hospital data into meaningful **KPIs, charts, and visual insights**, enabling hospital management to monitor operational efficiency, financial performance, staffing, patient flow, and quality of care.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze monthly patient volume trends
- Monitor Average Length of Stay (LOS)
- Analyze patient waiting time
- Evaluate bed occupancy across departments
- Identify departments with high and low performance
- Analyze staff hours and overtime
- Understand the relationship between staffing and patient wait time
- Compare department revenue and costs
- Analyze patient satisfaction
- Understand admission type patterns

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes important hospital KPIs such as:

- Total Patient Visits
- Average Length of Stay
- Average Wait Time
- Bed Occupancy Rate
- Total Revenue
- Total Visits
- Cost per Visit
- Average Patient Satisfaction

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains multiple visualizations for hospital performance analysis, including:

- Monthly Patient Volume & Length of Stay Trend
- Department-wise Visit Analysis
- Bed Occupancy by Department
- Top Departments by Patient Visits
- Overtime Analysis by Department
- Staff Hours vs. Patient Wait Time
- Revenue vs. Cost by Department
- Patient Satisfaction by Department
- Admission Type Analysis
- Department Performance Analysis

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard helps hospital management quickly identify operational trends and performance gaps.

It can be used to:

- Improve resource allocation
- Monitor department performance
- Optimize staffing levels
- Identify overtime patterns
- Reduce patient waiting time
- Monitor bed utilization
- Track financial performance
- Improve patient care and satisfaction
